This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal information when you use the MAEX PAY Android app. The app lets existing MAEX members sign in, view their membership profile, and change their password.

Information we handle

  • Sign-in and account security. When you sign in, the app sends your member code and password to the MAEX account service to verify your identity. If you change your password, the app sends your current password and the new password you enter and confirm so the account service can process the change.
  • Membership profile. The app retrieves the information available in your MAEX membership record. This can include your member ID and code, name, email address, phone number, National Registration Card (NRC) number, father’s name, date of birth, gender, address, profile photo, and registration date. The app uses this information to personalize your home screen and display your membership details.
  • Session information. The app stores your session token, its expiry time, and basic member information returned at sign-in in protected storage on your device so you can stay signed in. Your password is not saved on the device.

How information is transferred and shared

The app sends sign-in, profile, and password-change requests to the MAEX account service over HTTPS. If your membership record has a profile photo, the app requests it from the photo host over HTTPS without sending your MAEX session token.
